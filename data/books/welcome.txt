Welcome to Vix OS
===================

This is the built-in reader on your Xteink X3.

You are reading a plain-text (.txt) file. Use the arrow buttons to turn pages:
Down or Right goes forward, Up or Left goes back. Press Back to return to the
Library, and Back again for the home menu. Your reading position is saved
automatically for each book.


Adding your own books
---------------------

Three ways:

1. Over Wi-Fi: connect the device (Wi-Fi > Set up via phone), then choose
   Wi-Fi > Transfer files. On a phone on the same network, open the shown
   address (http://vix.local/) or scan the QR, and upload your files. Books go
   to the Library and images to Images automatically.

2. microSD card: copy .txt or .md files onto a card (root folder or a /books
   folder) and insert it. They appear in the Library tagged [SD].

3. Internal flash: put files in the firmware project's "data/books" folder and
   run:  pio run -t uploadfs


About this OS
-------------

Vix OS is a small, Duet-styled operating system: a home launcher for a set of
offline games, this book reader, plus Wi-Fi file transfer and QR tools. Every
screen shows the time, Wi-Fi signal and battery in the top bar. It runs on the
community e-paper SDK's display and input drivers, native to the X3's 792x528
e-ink screen.

Games: 2048, Tic-Tac-Toe, Sudoku, Minesweeper, 15 Puzzle, Lights Out, Snake,
Memory Match, Connect Four, Reversi, Hangman, and Blackjack.

Happy reading, and happy playing.
